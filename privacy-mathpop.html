01. Overview & Scope

MathPop is a single-player, offline math practice and brain-training game for Android, built by PlayPatch. This policy applies only to the MathPop application identified by the package name com.mathpop.game.

MathPop has no accounts, no login, no multiplayer, and no server backend of any kind. Everything the app does runs on your device, and everything it saves is stored on your device. The app does not request the INTERNET permission and performs no network communication.

Summary: We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or non-personal data through MathPop. There is no data for us to access, because none of it ever leaves your device.

02. What the App Does

MathPop is an offline arithmetic and math quiz suite. Its features are:

  • Levelled quiz gameplay: numbered Easy / Medium / Hard levels of multiple-choice math problems, with a par-time timer, correct/incorrect feedback, and step-by-step worked solutions. The question bank is procedurally extended once curated levels run out, so play is effectively endless.
  • Topics Hub: a reference and practice section covering arithmetic, algebra, geometry, trigonometry, statistics and more, with formulas, explanations, and topic-specific practice problems.
  • Math Quest mode: a custom quiz generator — you configure topic, difficulty, pacing, and time limit, and the app generates a quiz session.
  • Math Tricks library: a static reference screen of speed-calculation and mental-math shortcuts (e.g. Vedic math techniques).
  • Battle Mode: a single-player "race against an opponent" mode where you answer against a simulated computer-controlled bot. The bot's answers and timing are generated locally using simple randomized logic. There is no networking, matchmaking, or real opponent.
  • Scratchpad: an in-game freehand drawing canvas for working out math by hand during a level. Drawings exist only in on-screen memory while the level is open and are discarded when you leave the level — nothing is saved to disk or transmitted.
  • Stats screen: shows your own locally-tracked accuracy, average solve time, streaks, and level-completion history.
  • Settings: theme selection, sound on/off, and the option to redo the initial onboarding question.
  • Onboarding: a first-launch flow that asks your starting difficulty/experience level (a one-time in-app preference, not personal information) and shows a short tutorial.

03. Data Stored On Your Device

All persisted data is stored locally on your device only, using Android's standard SharedPreferences mechanism. None of it is transmitted anywhere — the app has no network permission and makes no network calls. Specifically, MathPop stores:

  • Selected color theme
  • Sound on/off preference
  • Whether onboarding has been completed, and the difficulty picked during onboarding
  • Per-level progress: which levels are completed, stars earned, best time, score, and per-level "in progress" state (current attempt, wrong answers tried, elapsed time) so an interrupted level can resume
  • Aggregate play statistics: current streak, best streak, total attempts, correct-answer count, total score
  • Achievement unlock state
  • "Next endless level" counters per difficulty tier (for the generated question bank once curated levels are exhausted)

None of this data identifies you personally. There are no user accounts, no names, no email addresses, and no device identifiers collected or stored by the app. The only way this data can leave your device is if you manually create your own device backup.

04. What We Do NOT Collect

  • No account registration, login, or authentication of any kind
  • No personal information (name, email, phone number, address, etc.)
  • No location data (no location permission requested)
  • No camera, microphone, or contacts access (no such permissions requested)
  • No advertising — the app ships ad-free; there is no ad SDK integrated (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob was removed from the project)
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind — no Firebase, no ad network, no analytics network, no social login
  • No in-app purchases or payment processing
  • No chat, messaging, or user-generated content shared with others (the Scratchpad is private, on-device, and ephemeral)

05. Android Permissions

None. The app's AndroidManifest.xml declares no <uses-permission> entries at all — not even INTERNET, since the app performs no network activity.

06. Third-Party Services & SDKs

None. MathPop's only dependencies are standard AndroidX / Jetpack Compose UI libraries and Kotlin's own serialization and coroutines libraries. All of these run entirely on-device and do not communicate with any server or collect any data. No third party receives any information from MathPop.

08. Children's Privacy

MathPop contains no mature content (no violence, profanity, gambling, or sexual content), no ads, no chat or social features, no purchases, and no data collection. It is a straightforward offline arithmetic quiz suitable for all ages, including children.

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children. We comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU GDPR, and Google Play's Families Policy requirements. If you have any concern about children's privacy in relation to MathPop, contact us using the details below.

09. Data Retention & Deletion

All MathPop data is local SharedPreferences data on your device. There is no server-side data to retain and nothing to separately request deletion of.

You can erase everything the app has stored at any time by either:

  • Clearing the app's storage: Android Settings → Apps → MathPop → Storage → Clear Data; or
  • Uninstalling the app.

If MathPop's data practices ever change (for example, if a future version adds an online feature), we will update this policy and revise the "Last Updated" date above before the change takes effect. Material changes will also be reflected in the app's Google Play Data Safety section.

User Data & Account Deletion

In accordance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion Policy, you have the right to request deletion of any associated data stored by PlayPatch games.

⚡ Quick Instant Device Data Deletion:

Most PlayPatch games store progress, unlocked skins, and settings purely on your local device. You can immediately wipe all local records at any time by going to your Android device Settings → Apps → [Game Name] → Storage → Clear Data & Cache, or by simply uninstalling the application.
